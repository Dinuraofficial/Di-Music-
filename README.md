## ✨ Features

### 🎧 Audio Playback
* Continuous background audio playback
* Android Media3 / ExoPlayer playback engine
* Lock screen media controls
* Notification playback controls
* Play, Pause, Next, Previous, and Seek controls
* Audio focus management
* YouTube Music stream resolution

<br />

### 🧭 Navigation & Interface
* Quick Picks dashboard
* Songs, Albums, Artists, and Playlists views
* Vertical navigation rail
* Material 3 interface
* Quick search access
* Light and Dark themes
* Edge-to-edge layout

<br />

### 🔍 Search
* Search songs, albums, artists, music videos, and playlists
* Featured and trending content
* Recent search history
* Dedicated Artist and Album pages
* One-tap song playback

<br />

### 🧠 Personalization & History
* Local listening history
* Recent search history
* Artist listening statistics
* Personalized Quick Picks
* Recommended songs and albums
* New-release discovery
* Genre-based fallback recommendations

<br />

### ⋯ Song Options
Available throughout the app:
* Play Next
* Add to Queue
* Add to Playlist
* Add / Remove from Favorites
* View Artist

<br />

### 🗂️ Playlists & Library
* Favorites management
* Create custom playlists
* Visual playlist selection
* Add/remove songs from playlists
* Play All & Shuffle All
* Reorder tracks
* Delete playlists

<br />

### 🎛️ Player
* Persistent Mini Player
* Full-screen "Now Playing" screen
* High-resolution artwork
* Ambient player background
* Interactive progress bar
* Playback position and duration display
* Shuffle and Repeat modes
* Interactive playback queue with reordering
* Synchronized / plain Lyrics view
* Direct Artist navigation

<br />

### 🎤 Artist & Album Pages
* High-res Artist artwork
* Artist information & subscriber/listener metrics
* Top songs list
* Play All and Shuffle buttons
* Complete discography & album details
* Release date and track count
* Currently playing track highlight
<br />

### ⚙️ Settings & Account
* Google / YouTube account login
* Account status
* Streaming quality preferences
* Theme and interface options
* Cache management
* Playback history management
